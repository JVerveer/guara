PROJECT NAME

VendorLens AI

Tagline:
Upload your compliance documents and instantly generate a DORA-ready vendor risk program.

PRODUCT VISION

Design a modern AI-powered SaaS platform for regulated financial institutions that transforms unstructured compliance documents into a complete DORA-ready third-party risk management program.

The product should feel like a combination of Stripe Dashboard, Linear, Vanta, Notion, and modern AI-native SaaS products.

The user uploads:

* Vendor lists
* Contracts
* Vendor questionnaires
* SOC reports
* ISO certificates
* Existing DORA registers
* Risk assessments
* Insurance certificates

The platform automatically generates:

* Vendor inventory
* Critical vendor inventory
* ICT third-party register
* Missing evidence report
* DORA gap analysis
* Concentration risk analysis
* Risk assessments
* Audit readiness score
* Audit package

CORE PRODUCT PROMISE

"Turn thousands of compliance documents into a complete DORA-ready vendor risk program in minutes."
